The Work

Four Rooms, Four Stories

Every project below carries the same signature: MR Interiors Design Studio spends more time listening than sketching, then builds a space that holds up to daily life, not just a photograph.

01

Lincoln Park Brownstone

A three-story greystone restored to its 1890s bones, then furnished for a family of five. We opened the kitchen to the garden, rebuilt the staircase in white oak and sourced every piece from Midwest ateliers.

  • Full Renovation
  • Kitchen
  • Millwork
02

Streeterville High-Rise

A 1,900-square-foot condo forty stories up needed warmth without clutter. Custom banquette seating, a stone bar and floor-to-ceiling drapery soften the skyline view.

  • Interior Architecture
  • Custom Furniture
03

Winnetka Family Home

A young family wanted a house that could survive muddy boots and formal dinners in the same week. We built a mudroom that works and a dining room that does not feel precious.

  • New Construction
  • Family Living
04

West Loop Loft

Exposed brick and steel beams stayed. Everything else we rebuilt around them: a kitchen in blackened steel, a bedroom wrapped in wool felt panels.

  • Loft Conversion
  • Lighting Design
