What we offer

Services built around how you actually live

Some clients need a full gut renovation, others just want a second opinion on a sofa. We built these six services to cover the whole range, and most projects end up pulling from more than one.

01

Full Renovations

Gut jobs and additions from permit set through final punch list. We coordinate with your contractor at every framing and finish decision so nothing gets value-engineered without you knowing.

02

Kitchen & Bath Design

Layout, cabinetry, stone, and plumbing fixtures specified down to the finish and hardware. We spend real time on storage and workflow before a single tile is chosen.

03

Space Planning

Furniture layouts, traffic flow, and lighting plans for a room or a whole floor. Useful before you buy anything, or when an existing room just isn't working.

04

Custom Furniture & Sourcing

Access to trade-only showrooms, custom upholstery, and local makers for casegoods and millwork. We handle ordering, freight, and delivery scheduling.

05

Styling Consultations

A two-hour session, in person or on a call, to work through what's already in the room. Art placement, textiles, and small swaps that change how a space reads.

06

Commercial Interiors

Offices, restaurants, and boutique retail spaces designed for durability and brand feel. We work directly with your build-out team on materials and code compliance.

Our approach

Design led by function, finished with restraint

We don't chase trends. A project earns its finishes when the layout is right first, the storage is sorted, and the light is considered. That's the order we work in, every time.

Founded by MR Interiors Design Studio in Chicago, we've spent over a decade on greystone renovations, condo remodels, and small commercial builds across the city and its near suburbs.
