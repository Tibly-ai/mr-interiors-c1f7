The Studio

Spaces built around how you actually live.

Founder

Mira Reyes

I started MR Interiors Design Studio in 2007 out of a two-desk office above a hardware store in Wicker Park, after six years drafting for a downtown architecture firm. I wanted to work directly with the people living in the rooms, not just the blueprints for them.

Eighteen years later, the studio has grown to a team of five, but the process hasn't changed much. We start with how a family actually moves through a house, morning coffee, homework at the counter, guests staying over, and let the design answer to that before it answers to a mood board.

We work in walnut, plaster, brass and linen more often than trend colors, because those materials age the way we want our projects to: better, quieter, with more character each year.

"A room is finished when you stop noticing the design and start noticing your life in it."
