The Studio

Design that gets built the way it was drawn

MR Interiors Design Studio has been shaping homes and small commercial spaces across Chicago since 2012. We're a small team, which means the person sketching your floor plan is the same one on site checking tile grout.

Every project starts with how a room is actually used, not just how it should photograph. We source furniture and materials with real lead times and real budgets in mind, and we stay involved through the last coat of paint.

What We Do

Four ways we work with clients

01

Full-Service Interior Design

Concept through install, for a single room or a whole house. We handle layout, materials, furniture, and site coordination.

02

Space Planning & Renovation

Floor plans, kitchen and bath layouts, and drawings we hand to your contractor, or one from our list of trusted crews.

03

Furniture & Sourcing

Access to trade-only showrooms and custom upholstery shops, with procurement and delivery scheduling handled for you.

04

Styling & Staging

Final layer work for finished rooms, plus staging for listings that need to sell fast and look their best in photos.
